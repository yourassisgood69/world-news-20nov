## Lead story

### 1. Trump, Xi and Putin issue a joint "peace declaration" — Shenzhen
- Met for 90 minutes on Thursday 19 November at Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, on
  the sidelines of the APEC summit — the first-ever trilateral meeting of
  the three leaders. The four-point "China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State
  Declaration toward Peace" was reported on 20 November.
- The declaration says "a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be
  fought" and promises dialogue on reducing nuclear risks and preventing
  proliferation, plus more oil supply, UN reform and respect for the
  immunities of state officials. It sets no timelines.
- It does NOT mention Russia's war in Ukraine or the US conflict with Iran.
- Sources: Reuters (via BusinessWorld, 20 Nov 2026); The Guardian;
  China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs text of the declaration.

## Story cards

### 2. World court: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
- The International Court of Justice ruled 9–6 on 19 November (reported
  20 November) that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in
  northern Rakhine State in 2017 — the first time the court has found a
  state responsible for committing genocide. It ordered safe, voluntary and
  dignified return of refugees and reparations.
- Sources: BBC/AP (via HK Businesswire, 20 Nov 2026); CNN, 19 Nov 2026;
  The Conversation, 20 Nov 2026.

### 3. Trump puts the Iran decision off to next year
- President Trump said on Thursday that he is "not rushing into any
  decision" on renewed strikes against Iran: "We'll see what happens
  after January 1." He told Reuters: "I'll talk to you about it after the
  beginning of the year." Reported 19–20 November.
- Sources: Reuters, 19 Nov 2026 ("Trump says US will 'see what happens'
  in conflict with Iran after January 1"); Axios via NY Post.

### 4. Anthropic's record debut jumps 17%
- Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share (above the $70–$78 range),
  raising a record $81.3bn on 980m shares; shares began trading on Nasdaq
  under ANT on 19 November, opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, up
  17% on the IPO price. The offering was due to close on Friday 20 November.
- Sources: Reuters (via BusinessWorld, 19 Nov 2026); Nasdaq; Motley Fool,
  19 Nov 2026.

### 5. Cyclone Akvan nears Odisha; 300,000 moved
- Severe cyclonic storm Akvan was due to make landfall near Gopalpur,
  Odisha, on Friday 20 November with winds of 90–100 km/h, gusting to
  110 km/h. Odisha evacuated more than 300,000 people (2,98,676 by
  Thursday evening, per the Special Relief Commissioner) to shelters.
- Note: the storm's name is Akvan, confirmed by the India Meteorological
  Department and ANI reporting on 20 November. No verified report of a
  storm called "Dana" for this event was found.
- Sources: ANI via Daily Prabhat, 20 Nov 2026; IMD bulletins; Times Now,
  20 Nov 2026.

### 6. Houthi strike hits Riyadh's tallest tower
- On Thursday 19 November (reported 20 November), a Houthi attack struck
  the PIF Tower — Riyadh's tallest skyscraper and HQ of the Public
  Investment Fund, the Saudi sovereign wealth fund — in the King Abdullah
  Financial District. Saudi civil defence said it caused material damage
  to several floors and nearby buildings; windows were blown out across
  seven to eight floors; no injuries were reported. The second strike on
  the district in under two weeks (Aramco's building was hit on 8 November).
- Sources: Reuters (via Devdiscourse/Sowetan, 20 Nov 2026); Saudi Press
  Agency, 19 Nov 2026.

### 7. GTA VI takes an estimated $1.84bn on day one
- Grand Theft Auto VI launched on 19 November. Sensor Tower estimates
  19.05m copies sold by launch day, worth about $1.84bn — passing the
  entire $1.805bn worldwide box-office run of Christopher Nolan's
  "The Odyssey". The figures are third-party estimates, not official
  Take-Two/Rockstar sales data.
- Sources: Sensor Tower via Forbes, 19 Nov 2026; Fox 5 NY; 80.lv.

## Also today

### 8. COP31 enters its closing day in Antalya with no deal yet
- The climate summit's scheduled closing day is Friday 20 November.
  A revised draft published on Thursday cut the cover decision from 144
  paragraphs to 80, dropping all fossil-fuel language and the $120bn-a-year
  adaptation-finance figure from the first draft (16 November). No final
  agreement had been reached as the last day began.
- Sources: The Hindu, 20 Nov 2026; AsiaToday, 20 Nov 2026; Reuters.

### 9. NASA confirms Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth
- Voyager 1 reached one light-day from Earth (about 16.1bn miles /
  25.9bn km) at 5:16 a.m. EST on 18 November; NASA confirmed the
  milestone on 19 November, when the probe's signal — travelling at light
  speed — arrived. It is the first human-made object to get that far.
- Sources: NASA, 19 Nov 2026; Reuters, 19 Nov 2026; Smithsonian Magazine.

### 10. Russia strikes Kyiv with ballistic missiles overnight
- Russia attacked Kyiv with ballistic missiles overnight into 20 November,
  triggering explosions and fires in several districts; air-raid alerts
  were issued in the capital and other regions.
- Sources: h.ua / Kyiv Independent live reporting, 20 Nov 2026.

### 11. Drones halt Genoa airport flights for about two hours
- Genova Cristoforo Colombo Airport suspended flights shortly before
  20:00 on Thursday 19 November after unauthorised drones were spotted
  near the runway, and resumed shortly after 22:00 — about two hours.
- Sources: Corriere della Sera / Il Messaggero via World Today Journal,
  19 Nov 2026.

### 12. JAXA drops Hayabusa2's 2031 asteroid landing after engine trouble
- JAXA announced on 17 November (reported 19 November) that ion-engine
  trouble forced it to abandon the planned 2031 rendezvous and landing
  attempt on asteroid 1998 KY26. Hayabusa2 will instead make a high-speed
  flyby of asteroid 2015 BM510 in April 2028.
- Sources: JAXA press briefing, 17 Nov 2026; The Japan Times / Space.com,
  19 Nov 2026.
